3. **Exact Page Provenance Preserved:** Physical PDF page numbers where names actually appear on paper are preserved for every individual (for example, Habtu's children `ባሻይ ተኪኤ` and `ሓጎስ` are recorded on Page 9, while `ገብረ`, `ለትብርሃን`, and `ትብለጽ` are recorded on Page 10).

5. **Repeated Names Integrity:** All 92 repeated-name groups (274 occurrences) are preserved as distinct individuals with unique, immutable IDs (`#pers_p...`), strictly adhering to family naming traditions without merging.

## 5. Handling of Repeated Names

Under strict family data integrity guidelines:
* **Repeated Names Are Expected:** In Tigrinya/Eritrean family naming tradition, children and grandchildren are frequently named in honor of grandparents and ancestors.
* **Zero Merging:** The 92 shared-name groups (comprising 274 individual occurrences) are **never merged**. Each person record retains an immutable, unique identifier (`#pers_p...`).
* **Informational Reporting:** The Shared Names report in the application is strictly an informational tool for disambiguation, not a list of errors requiring deduplication.
* **Identity Context:** Individuals sharing the same name are distinguished by their source page, generation level, parent lineage, and branch.
